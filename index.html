What makes it different

📄

A single, free-flowing view

There are no pages, sections, or documents to create and switch between. Everything you've ever written lives on one endless page, newest first. Open the app and start typing — that's the whole interface.

🏷

Structure comes from what you type

Drop a #tag or a date like @2026-01-15 anywhere in a line and the sidebar fills itself in. Browse by tag (with nested #parent/child tags) or by month and day. Nothing to file, move, or reorganize — the structure is derived from your words, never imposed on them.

#ideas#1:1#work/urgent@2026-01-15@01/15/2026
🔄

Seamlessly synced to your Drive

Connect Google Drive once. From then on every change syncs both ways every few minutes, into a plain Notes Diary/ folder you own. Pick up on your phone, laptop, or a new browser and your diary is already there.

🔒

Local-first and private

Your writing is stored in your browser and works fully offline — no account, no server holding your data. Every project is protected by a password that encrypts your entries on your device. Drive sync is optional, uses your own account, and you can disconnect it any time.

📅

Your Google Calendar, beside your notes

Open a calendar pane next to your diary to see the past week and the next 30 days. Filter by RSVP status, jump to the meeting happening now, join video calls in one click, and insert an event's link straight into a note.

⏱

Upcoming and Past at a glance

Put a date like @2026-01-15 on a line and switch between Upcoming and Past tabs to see what's next or what's behind you. Combine with tags and search to narrow further. Task lists render as real, clickable checkboxes.

🖼

Paste images right into a note

Paste or drag a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP into any note and it's uploaded to your own Drive folder and shown as a thumbnail. Images are not encrypted — anyone with access to that Drive folder can see them.

🗂

Multiple projects, your backups

Keep separate diaries — work, personal, a trip — each with its own password and Drive folder. Split a project into backup files with filter rules, download as JSON or Markdown, and import into a new project from a file or from Drive.

How it works

Write

Type into the one page. Markdown renders as you go. Add #tags and dates like @2026-01-15 inline whenever they're useful — or don't.

Browse

The left rail builds itself from your tags and dates. Click a tag or a month to filter the stream; combine them to narrow further.

Sync

Connect Drive and every device stays in step automatically. Archive instead of delete, and export anytime as JSON or Markdown.
